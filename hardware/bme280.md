# GY-BME280-5 / BME280 Environmental Sensor

## Role

The GY-BME280-5 is the initial Kobo-WX environmental sensor module.

## Measurements

The BME280 provides all three primary measurements needed for the first Kobo-WX build:

- Temperature
- Relative humidity
- Atmospheric pressure

Software can derive additional weather values such as dew point. Pressure can also be presented in hPa and inHg, with local sea-level/QNH correction explored later.

## Module

The acquired part was sold/listed as:

**BME280 5V Atmospheric Pressure Sensor GY-BME280-5 Temperature Humidity Sensor Module**

Before wiring, we will inspect the actual board markings and verify its pin labels, supply arrangement, and I2C connections rather than relying only on the seller title.

## Interface

The BME280 supports I2C communication, which is the intended interface for the first Kobo-WX prototype.

## First milestone

Confirm that the ESP32 can detect the BME280 over I2C and repeatedly read stable temperature, humidity, and pressure values.
